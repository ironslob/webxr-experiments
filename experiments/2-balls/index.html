Balls

Quest 3 passthrough. Enter AR drops colored balls into the room scan. They bounce off the surfaces you pick. Turn gravity off to keep them floating. Controllers can poke, grab and throw, or swing baseball bats.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page.
  • Controllers In poke mode, push through a ball to knock it. In grab mode, hold trigger near a ball to pick it up, release to throw. With a bat size set, swing the bats into balls to hit them.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

The controls above are the ones inside the headset.
