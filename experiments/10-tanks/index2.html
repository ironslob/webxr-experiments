Tanks

Quest 3 VR tank commander. Pick a procedural tank, drive over rolling grassy hills, switch between the cockpit and a chase view, swing the turret, and fire on wandering AI tanks. A radar in the corner keeps them spotted. Scoring and return fire can come later — this is the simulator core.

The controls below are the ones inside the headset.

Controls

  • Enter VR Starts the immersive session from this page. On desktop without VR, falls back to a keyboard preview.
  • Left stick Drive the hull only — throttle and steer. Movement follows the tracks, not the turret.
  • Right stick Traverse the turret and elevate the gun independently of the hull.
  • Trigger Fire the main cannon.
  • Squeeze Hold to fire the coaxial machine gun.
  • A or X Toggle cockpit and third-person chase view.
  • Radar Corner map, heading-up: you are the wedge, enemies are red blips.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.
  • Desktop preview WASD drive hull, arrows turn turret, Space cannon, F machine gun, V view, Esc exit.
