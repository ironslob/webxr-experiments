Tanks

Quest 3 VR tank commander. Pick a procedural tank and drive rolling grassy hills — the hull sits on the slope under the tracks. Turning the hull turns your view with it, in the hatch and behind the tank. An amber sight marks where the gun is pointed, and a red diamond marks each enemy tank. A dotted line along the cannon shows where a shot will go. A radar in the corner keeps the tanks spotted.

The controls below are the ones inside the headset.

Controls

  • Enter VR Starts the immersive session from this page. On desktop without VR, falls back to a keyboard preview.
  • Left stick Drive and steer the hull. The view yaws with the hull, so the hills swing around you as you turn. Movement follows the tracks, not the turret.
  • Right stick Traverse the turret and elevate the gun. Up raises the barrel, down lowers it. Aim stays independent of the hull.
  • Trigger Fire the main cannon.
  • Squeeze Hold to fire the coaxial machine gun.
  • A or X Toggle cockpit and chase view. Both stay locked to the hull, so turning the tank turns the view.
  • Stick click or L Show or hide the dotted cannon line. Either thumbstick click works in the headset. L does it in the desktop preview.
  • Sight Cockpit only. The amber target rides on the gun, so traverse and elevation walk it onto a bullseye or an enemy diamond.
  • Radar Corner map. You are the wedge, enemies are red blips. Tank nose keeps the hull's forward at the top. Headset keeps the direction you are looking at the top.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.
  • Desktop preview WASD drive hull, arrows turn turret, Space cannon, F machine gun, V view, L cannon line, Esc exit.
