Walls

Quest 3 passthrough. Enter AR stands you on the floor, reads the room scan, and paints each wall with its own translucent color. A laptop can open this page, but it cannot start the session.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

The controls above are the ones inside the headset.
