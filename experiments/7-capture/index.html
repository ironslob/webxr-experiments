Capture

Builds a textured model of your room on Quest 3. Geometry comes from the Space Setup mesh; colour is baked from the headset cameras as you look around, gated by depth sensing when the browser provides it. Finish and passthrough drops away so you stand inside the capture.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page. Camera permission follows after.
  • Look around Hold still briefly on each surface. Keyframes fire automatically when the head settles.
  • Squeeze Force a keyframe from the current view, even while moving.
  • Trigger or A Finishes the bake and drops you into the opaque captured room.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

Space Setup mesh has no textures in WebXR. This page paints colour from the Quest cameras onto that mesh, using depth to reject bad samples.
