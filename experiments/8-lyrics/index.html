Lyrics

Quest 3 passthrough. Play a mix out loud and the words drop in front of you on the beat. Close a fist on one — your hand, or the grip of a controller — and pull it down into the ring at your chest. It goes into you and shoots back up as a firework. The more lyrics you pull in, the higher and wider that display climbs.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page and opens the mic or demo beat.
  • Fist With hand tracking, close your fist on a word to grab it. Open your hand to let go.
  • Squeeze On a controller, squeeze the grip — or pull the trigger — to grab the word in your fist.
  • Pull in Draw the word down into the ring at your chest. It bursts upward from that ring. Each word you feed makes the firework taller, wider, and busier.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

The ring sits at your chest and the firework stays in front of you. Paste different words before you enter if you want the mix’s lyrics.
