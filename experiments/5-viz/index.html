Viz

Quest 3 passthrough. Geometric shapes and a few models float in the room and dance to sound — pulse, stretch, spin, flash color, jump on beats. Each object gets a random mix of reactions. Play music near the headset, or use the built-in demo beat.

Models Used when Mix includes models. Leave at least one on.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page and opens the mic or demo beat.
  • Sound Play music near the headset, or pick Demo beat. Objects pulse, stretch, spin, recolor, and jump on hits.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

The controls above are the ones inside the headset.
