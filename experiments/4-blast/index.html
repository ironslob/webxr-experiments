Blast

Quest 3 passthrough. Drop a mix of models into the room scan, pick their size, then poke or bat them. A solid hit turns one into a firework and respawns it somewhere else at a random size.

Models Which meshes can spawn. Leave at least one on.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page.
  • Controllers In poke mode, push through an object to blast it. In grab mode, hold trigger near one to pick it up, release to throw. With a bat size set, swing into objects to blast them. Each blast respawns at a random place, velocity, and size.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

The controls above are the ones inside the headset.
