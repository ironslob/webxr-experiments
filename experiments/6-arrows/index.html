Arrows

Quest 3 passthrough. Arrows crawl along scanned floors, walls, and ceilings. They wander and turn at random. Loud sound makes them pulse and shift color. Move around a lot and they scatter. Hit a wall from the floor and they climb it; hit a floor or ceiling from a wall and they keep going along that surface.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page and opens the mic or demo beat.
  • Sound Play music or make noise near the headset. Arrows pulse and change color with the energy.
  • Move Walk or swing your head hard and the arrows scatter into new directions.
  • B or Y Leaves the session. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.

The controls above are the ones inside the headset.
