Slice

Quest 3 passthrough. A katana sits in one hand, or one in each, and targets arc through the room. Fruit is apples, oranges, watermelon, bananas, strawberries, and pineapples. Animals is a flock led by sheep, plus chickens, pigs, rabbits, ducks, and penguins. Cosmos throws planets, moons, stars, comets, and asteroids. Every cut hangs its points in the air. The left board keeps the running score. The right board is that mode’s high score table.

Controls

  • Enter AR Starts the session from this page. The round uses the settings above.
  • Swing Flick a blade through a target. Resting the sword on it does not count. Each sword slices on its own.
  • Score The points appear where you cut. A clean cut through the middle is worth more, and quick chains raise the combo. The left board keeps the total. The right board is this mode’s high scores.
  • Hazards Fruit hides bombs, animals hide skunks, cosmos hides black holes. Slicing one subtracts points and clears the combo.
  • Practice On a computer, where immersive AR is unavailable, Practice on this screen swings a sword with the mouse. Escape or Exit leaves and saves the score.
  • B or Y Leaves the session and saves the round if it ranks. B is on the right controller, Y on the left.
  • Meta Opens the system menu on the right controller. Use it if B or Y will not close the session.
